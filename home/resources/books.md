The Goalie Guild founder writes a series of educational and motivational books for goaltenders called [**The Power Within**](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B08SMHQ79D). Co-authored alongside Mike Valley, this series focuses on sharing wisdom and insights from NHL goalies about the mental game. Additional books written by Goldman include Between Two Worlds and The Periodic Table of Goaltending Elements.&#x20;

## [The Power Within III](https://www.amazon.com/Power-Within-III-Elevating-Goaltending/dp/B0DQ5Y4TNK)

Elite goaltending isn’t about perfection – it’s about how you process the imperfect moments. It’s about training your mind to find clarity in chaos, to embrace pressure rather than fight it, and to see the game unfold before it happens. Because while your body makes the saves, it’s your mind that wins the battles. **The Power Within III** is the fourth installment in a series that is dedicated to sharing valuable life lessons from well-known NHL goalies and goalie coaches. By having them share some of their favorite stories from the crease, we aim to empower goalies of all ages to become the best version of themselves.

## [The Power Within II](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B08SGVNYZS)

Through another collection of candid conversations with masters of the goaltending position, this book is a perfect guide and companion for anyone on the path to puck-stopping greatness. Each chapter is full of wisdom and insights from NHL and Olympic goalies on the mental game, as well as personal stories and valuable life lessons that will elevate your thinking and help take your game to new heights. The book is dedicated to Michel Goldman, who tragically passed away shortly before the book was published.

## [Embracing The Grind](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/B01N9I3Z24)

The second book in the trilogy was published in January of 2017. It continued to build on the Three Pillars of Elite Goaltending concept and included a more serious look at the pressures of playing the position at the highest levels. It featured Ben Bishop, Freddy Brathwaite, Scott Darling, Jhonas Enroth, Johan Hedberg, Jimmy Howard, Mitch Korn, Drew MacIntyre, Curtis McElhinney, Evgeni Nabokov, and Jordan Sigalet.

## [The Power Within I](https://www.amazon.com/Power-Within-Discovering-Elite-Goaltending-ebook/dp/B00O2RPEE4)

The first book in the trilogy was published in January of 2013. It introduced the <mark style="color:red;">Three Pillars of Elite Goaltending</mark> concept and included interviews with NHL goalies and goalie coaches. It featured Pekka Rinne, Niklas Backstrom, Marty Turco, Tomas Vokoun, Brian Elliott, Richard Bachman, and Chris Mason. It also includes interviews with two goalie coaches, Mitch Korn and Erik Granqvist.

## [Between Two Worlds](https://www.amazon.com/Between-Two-Worlds-Discovering-Development-ebook/dp/B019HB3YZU)

Justin's coaching manifesto was the result of a four-month summer journey to Finland, Canada, and throughout the United States in order to discover new realms of hockey goalie development. Traveling over 24,000 miles and scouting more than 250 goalies from May until September of 2014, Goldman was exposed to a plethora of different coaching methods, training environments, and tactical puck-stopping strategies. Interviews include Jukka Ropponen, Hannu Nykvist, Thomas Magnusson, Mitch Korn, Corey Hirsch, Jack Hartigan, Eli Wilson, and many more.
