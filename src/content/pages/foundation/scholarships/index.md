## Introduction

The Goalie Guild offers a variety of annual **Scholarships** for the goalie community.&#x20;

## Mission

Our annual Scholarships are dedicated to supporting underprivileged goalies and their families, especially those without access to training, support, or basic education. By offering goalies in need with training, mentoring, and financial aid for other goaltending purposes, we can empower athletes to embrace the many benefits of a healthy, active lifestyle.

## How It Works

Anyone is welcome to apply for our annual Scholarships. Applicants should be goaltenders (or their parents/guardians) or goalie coaches from the United States.

Each application is reviewed on an individual basis. The review process can take anywhere from 2-4 weeks. In almost every instance, additional information related to the recipient is gathered and discussed before a final decision is made. Thank you for being patient during our review process.

Upon approval, a scholarship recipient will be rewarded with a donation from our foundation.&#x20;

## How to Apply

Send a cover letter and request directly to us at: <mark>**apply@thegoalieguild.com**</mark>
