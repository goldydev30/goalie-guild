## _**To educate, support, and inspire the goaltending community.**_  <a href="#mission-statement" id="mission-statement"></a>

The Goalie Guild is a 501-c3 nonprofit foundation dedicated to supporting the goaltending community through accessible, affordable, and professional development resources like [Scholarships](/../../../../../foundation/scholarships/) and the [GGR](/../../../../../resources/ggr/) event.&#x20;

Our mission is supported by our long-term [Vision](/../../../../../foundation/intro/vision/).
