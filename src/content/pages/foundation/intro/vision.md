## _**To build and maintain an independent foundation that serves and supports the global goaltending community.**_&#x20;

Made in the image and likeness of a [Medieval knight's Guild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guild), The Goalie Guild is dedicated to being a builder in the goaltending community. We accomplish this by connecting and bridging hockey nations together, guiding players, and hosting professional development events for coaches.&#x20;
