## Intro

The individuals listed below are licensed mental health care providers and professional mental skills coaches with a relevant background in goaltending. As such, they are tremendous resources for goalies of all ages that are seeking support.&#x20;

Please feel free to contact them at any time if you are interested in talking to them or using any of their services. If you would like to request a $100 voucher from Lift The Mask, please [submit this form](https://liftthemask.com/) on Lift the Mask.
