## Introduction

Below is a collection of our <mark>Online Toolkits</mark>.

Online Toolkits are curated "living documents" that include relevant and emerging research on goaltending development and skill acquisition. The toolkits are initially distributed to the guests of our [Global Goaltending Retreat](/resources/ggr/) and then shared publicly at the beginning of the next season.&#x20;

If you are interested in any of our ongoing research, please contact us directly.
